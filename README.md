# Royal Bridges General Services Marketplace

A general services marketplace connecting customers and companies with traders, licensed freelancers, service providers and businesses.

## Marketplace model

- Service providers can register and advertise their services.
- Customers and companies can post requirements and enquiries.
- Suitable providers can respond to relevant opportunities.
- Businesses may purchase access to suitable enquiries, subject to the marketplace's commercial terms.

## Service categories

The marketplace is intended to cover a broad range of services, including:

- Yacht trips and yacht rentals
  - Private yacht charters
  - Shared yacht trips
  - Sunset cruises
  - Birthday, celebration and special-event packages
  - Corporate and group bookings

Additional service categories can be added as the marketplace develops.

## Project separation

This General Services Marketplace is separate from the Royal Bridges Real Estate, Relocation, Direct Property and Consultancy websites. Relevant enquiries may later be routed into the private Royal Bridges CRM.
