HOW IT WORKS

A straightforward route
from need to connection.

The marketplace is designed to make service opportunities easier to describe, discover and respond to.

01 · CLIENTS

Describe what you need

Share the service category, location, timing, scope and any important requirements. Avoid including sensitive personal details in the public description.

02 · MARKETPLACE REVIEW

Requirements are checked

Submissions can be reviewed for clarity, suitability and any required credentials before being made available to providers.

03 · PROVIDERS

Relevant providers respond

Registered providers can review opportunities that match their service area and submit an expression of interest.

04 · CLIENT CHOICE

Shortlist and connect

Clients can compare suitable responses and decide which provider or providers they want to contact.

IMPORTANT

Professional standards matter.

Providers must hold any trade licence, professional authorisation, insurance or other approvals required for the work they offer. Regulated legal, medical, financial, immigration and other specialist services must only be delivered by appropriately authorised professionals. The platform does not itself grant permission to perform regulated work.
